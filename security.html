Security and data handling

What happens to your fund's data when we build for you. Each commitment below is a numbered term in our client agreement, and your counsel will see the same wording.

CommitmentWhat it means in practiceAgreement
Runs in your environmentWe build, run and store everything in your cloud account or on-prem. We copy data to our own systems only when you approve a specific task in writing, and delete that copy when the task is done.§4.6
Model providers you approveData goes only to model providers you have approved, under terms that forbid training on it, with zero retention where the provider offers it. On request we use your own provider accounts, or models hosted entirely in your environment.§4.7
No trainingYour data, and anything derived from it, is never used to train, tune or evaluate any model, ours or a third party's. The only exception is a model you ask us to tune that runs solely for you inside your environment.§4.3
No sharing, no resaleNothing from your fund is disclosed to or used for any other client. Your data is kept separate and never sold or licensed, including in aggregated or anonymized form.§4.4–4.5
Only the people who need itAccess is limited to the engineers on your engagement, under written confidentiality terms and trained on handling material non-public information. Access is removed when it's no longer needed.§4.9
No subprocessors without consentNo third party processes your data unless you agree in writing, on terms at least as strict as ours.§4.8
You hear firstIf there's a security incident, or a subpoena or regulator asks for your data, we tell you promptly and within a deadline set in the agreement.§4.11–4.12
You own what we buildCode, prompts, test sets and outputs are assigned to you, with a permanent license to any reusable parts of ours inside them.§7
Returned, deleted, certifiedAt the end of the engagement, or whenever you ask, your data is returned and every copy we hold is deleted. We certify the deletion in writing. A sample is below.§4.13

We answer your security and due-diligence questionnaire before a pilot starts, and provide evidence of these controls once a year on request (§4.14).

A sample deletion certificate

This is the document you receive when an engagement ends. It lists every place your data was held, how it was removed, and who checked.

Your compliance team can file it with the rest of your vendor records. If the law requires us to keep anything, the certificate says what and why, and that copy stays under the same terms.
