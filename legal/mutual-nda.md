# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

>
> *Use this before any detailed conversation with a prospect. Many funds will send their own NDA instead; that's fine. Check theirs against Sections 2, 4 and 6 below. Keep it short: a long NDA slows down the first meeting.*

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of [Effective Date] between **Deskside [LLC / Inc.]**, a [State] [entity type] with offices at [Address] (**"Deskside"**), and **[Counterparty legal name]**, a [jurisdiction and entity type] with offices at [Address] (**"Counterparty"**). Each is a **"Party."**

## 1. Purpose

The Parties want to discuss a possible engagement in which Deskside would build research and analysis tools for Counterparty (the **"Purpose"**). To do so, each may share Confidential Information with the other.

## 2. Confidential Information

2.1 **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information disclosed by one Party (the **"Discloser"**) to the other (the **"Recipient"**), in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances. For Counterparty, this includes its positions, portfolio and trading information, strategies, research, models, investors, vendors, systems, and security practices. For Deskside, this includes its non-public methods, code, pricing, and product plans.

2.2 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show:
  (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient;
  (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure;
  (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or
  (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:
  (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
  (b) disclose it only to its employees, members, and professional advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;
  (c) protect it with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care; and
  (d) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or disclosure it becomes aware of.

## 4. Securities law matters

4.1 **No trading.** Deskside acknowledges that Counterparty's Confidential Information may include material non-public information. Deskside and its personnel will not trade in any security, or advise or encourage anyone else to trade, on the basis of Counterparty's Confidential Information, and will comply with applicable securities laws.

4.2 **Share only what's needed.** *Before an engagement, Deskside does not need positions or portfolio data.* Counterparty agrees not to share material non-public information about any issuer during discussions under this Agreement. If it is shared by mistake, the Recipient will notify the Discloser and follow its reasonable instructions.

4.3 **Not an adviser.** Deskside is not an investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing exchanged under this Agreement is investment advice.

## 5. No data for any other purpose

Deskside will not use Counterparty's Confidential Information to train, tune, or evaluate any AI model, or for any other client, prospect, or product. *This mirrors the privacy promise in MSA Section 4 so prospects hear the same thing from the first document they sign.*

## 6. Compelled disclosure

If the Recipient is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose Confidential Information, it will, to the extent legally permitted, give the Discloser prompt notice and a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order, and disclose only the minimum required.

## 7. Return and deletion

On the Discloser's written request, or when discussions end, the Recipient will return or securely delete the Discloser's Confidential Information and, on request, confirm deletion in writing. The Recipient may keep a copy only if required by law or in routine backups that are not readily accessible; any retained copy stays subject to this Agreement.

## 8. No license, no obligation

No license or other right is granted except as stated here. Neither Party is obliged to enter into any further agreement. All information is provided "as is."

## 9. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made within [2] years of the Effective Date. The obligations in Sections 3 to 7 last for [3] years after the last disclosure, and for trade secrets and information about Counterparty's positions, strategies, or investors, for as long as the information remains confidential.

## 10. General

10.1 **Remedies.** Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm. The Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.

10.2 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [New York], without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The courts in [New York County, New York] have exclusive jurisdiction.

10.3 **Entire agreement.** This is the entire agreement on its subject. It may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. It may be signed electronically and in counterparts. If the Parties later sign a Master Services Agreement, its confidentiality terms govern information shared under it.
