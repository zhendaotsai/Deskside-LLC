## Our privacy promise, in one place

These commitments appear in the MSA (Section 4) and in plain English in the privacy policy. Don't weaken one without the other.

1. Client data is used only for that client's work.
2. It is never used to train or improve any AI model.
3. It is never shared with or used for another client, and never sold, even anonymized.
4. We run inside the client's environment by default.
5. Model providers are used only under no-training terms, with zero retention where available.
6. No new subprocessors without the client's consent.
7. Breaches are reported within 48 hours.
8. Data is returned and deleted within 30 days of the end, with written certification.
9. We never trade on, or leak, client information or MNPI. Information barriers separate clients.
10. We never use a client's name without written permission.
11. The website has no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party requests (fonts are self-hosted).

## Decisions to make before sending

- **Entity details:** the LLC's state of formation, registered address, and signatory.
- **Governing law and venue:** the drafts assume New York.
- **Liability:** the general cap (12 months of fees) and the data/confidentiality supercap. Set the supercap at a level your insurance actually covers.
- **Insurance:** tech E&O, cyber, and general liability limits. Funds commonly ask for $1–5M. Get quotes before promising amounts.
- **Breach notice window:** 48 hours is drafted. Some funds will ask for 24.
- **Payment terms:** a 50/50 split for fixed fees and net-30 invoices.
- **SOC 2:** keep the roadmap line only if you'll actually pursue it.
- **Exclusivity:** off by default. Offer it per SOW for a price, and never across a whole sector.

## Keep the promises true

- **Personal trading policy:** write one before your first engagement (MSA 5.2) and have both founders sign it.
- **Company devices:** use company-managed devices with disk encryption and MFA everywhere (Exhibit B).
- **Mailbox retention:** set up `hello@desksidelabs.com` with the retention described in the privacy policy (24 months for inquiries).
- **New website tools:** if you ever add analytics, a form service, or embedded scheduling (e.g. Calendly), update the privacy policy first. The "no third parties" line would no longer be true.
- **Prior work:** don't reuse code, prompts, or data from prior employers. The MSA warrants that nothing prevents you from assigning the Deliverables (10.2(d)).

## What funds will send you

Expect their own paper: an NDA, a vendor security questionnaire (often SIG Lite or a custom one), sometimes their own MSA, and an expert-network or MNPI attestation. This MSA is designed to survive that process. Where they insist on their form, use Section 4 and Exhibit B as your checklist for what must be in it.
