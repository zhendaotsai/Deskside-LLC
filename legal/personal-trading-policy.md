# Personal Trading and Confidential Information Policy

> **DRAFT for review by counsel or a compliance consultant. This is not legal advice.** Square-bracketed items `[like this]` are decisions to make. Notes in *italics* should be deleted before signing.
>
> *Required by MSA Section 5.2 before the first client engagement. Both founders sign it, and so does anyone who later works on client engagements. Funds will ask to see it.*

**Effective date:** [__]  **Applies to:** every Deskside founder, employee and contractor (**"Personnel"**), and accounts they control or benefit from, including accounts of a spouse, partner, or dependants living with them (**"Covered Accounts"**).

## 1. Why this exists

We see our clients' research, positions, and conversations. Clients trust us not to trade on that, leak it, or let it move between clients. This policy keeps that promise and keeps us inside securities law.

## 2. Never trade on client or non-public information

2.1 Personnel must not trade any security, or advise or encourage anyone else to trade, on the basis of:
  (a) any client's Confidential Information or Client Data, including positions, orders, research, models, or opinions; or
  (b) any material non-public information (**"MNPI"**), however obtained.

2.2 Do not share client information with anyone who does not need it for that client's engagement, including other Deskside Personnel working for other clients.

## 3. Restricted list

3.1 Deskside keeps a **restricted list** of issuers. An issuer goes on it when Personnel:
  (a) learn of a client's position or interest in it during an engagement *[simplest approach: restrict every issuer in any active client's coverage list]*; or
  (b) come across possible MNPI about it.

3.2 Personnel may not trade restricted issuers in Covered Accounts, including options, swaps and other derivatives on them.

3.3 Issuers stay on the list until [the engagement ends plus [90] days / compliance removes them].

## 4. Pre-clearance and holding period

4.1 Before any trade in an individual security in a Covered Account, Personnel must get written pre-clearance from [the other founder / designated compliance person]. Approval lasts [1] trading day.

4.2 Exempt (no pre-clearance needed): broad-based mutual funds, ETFs, and index funds; government bonds; accounts where the person has no investment discretion (a documented managed account).

4.3 Minimum holding period: [30] days, unless pre-cleared for hardship.

## 5. Reporting

5.1 On joining and every year: a list of Covered Accounts and holdings.

5.2 Every quarter: a statement of transactions in Covered Accounts, within [30] days of quarter-end.

5.3 Where a client requires it (MSA 5.2), Personnel on that engagement also follow the client's own pre-clearance and reporting rules.

## 6. MNPI during engagements

6.1 If you think you have received MNPI (for example, on an expert or management call), stop, don't pass it on, and tell the client's compliance contact and [designated person] the same day. Add the issuer to the restricted list.

6.2 Automated MNPI flags in our tools support compliance; they don't replace it.

## 7. Information barriers between clients

7.1 Client Data stays in that client's environment. Personnel don't carry client information, notes, or code between client engagements.

7.2 Work for competing funds is allowed only with separate access, separate storage, and no sharing of client information, as required by MSA Section 5.4.

## 8. Employer obligations

Personnel who also have another employer must follow that employer's policies too. Where the two conflict, the stricter rule applies. Never use an employer's information, systems, or contacts for Deskside.

## 9. Breaches

Report any breach, including your own, to [designated person] immediately. Breaches may lead to removal from engagements and termination, and may have to be reported to clients or regulators.

## 10. Records

Deskside keeps pre-clearance requests, approvals, reports, and the restricted list for [5] years.
