Services

Engineers who ship inside your investment workflow.

Every engagement starts with a real workflow and a real user. Here's where we usually start, and how we structure the work.

01

Proactive agents

Not every useful agent waits to be asked. We build agents that run continuously in your environment, watching filings, prices, data feeds, positions, and inboxes. They act when something changes: they do the first pass, attach the evidence, and bring it to the right person. You set what they watch, when they escalate, and which actions always need a human to approve.

  • Filing and news alerts on your positions
  • Pre-open briefs, built overnight
  • Risk and exposure limit monitoring
  • Late or anomalous vendor-data detection
  • Trade-break triage with drafted fixes
  • Inbox agents for fund-admin and broker email
02

Research & analyst tools

Your analysts spend hours reading so they can spend minutes thinking. We build agents that do the reading — across filings, transcripts, broker research, expert-network calls, and your own internal notes — and bring back what changed, with citations.

  • Earnings-call and filing diffing
  • Coverage-universe morning briefs
  • Search over internal research notes
  • Expert-call summarization and tagging
  • Model-update assistants for Excel
  • Thesis tracking and catalyst calendars
03

Data & alternative data

Structured signals hide in unstructured sources. We build pipelines that extract, clean, and validate data from documents, websites, and vendor feeds, and put it where your quants and fundamental teams already work. Our quant research background means we check point-in-time correctness, look-ahead bias, and coverage before anyone builds a signal on it.

  • Document-to-dataset extraction
  • Vendor-data evaluation and onboarding
  • Entity resolution and ticker mapping
  • KPI tracking from company disclosures
  • Data-quality monitoring and alerting
  • Natural-language access to your warehouse
04

Operations & investor relations

The middle and back office run on email, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge. We automate the repetitive parts with human review built in, so your ops team spends time on exceptions — not on copying numbers.

  • Reconciliation and trade-break triage
  • DDQ and RFP response drafting
  • Investor letter and report prep
  • Counterparty and fund-admin email handling
  • Onboarding and KYC document review
  • Internal knowledge assistant
05

Compliance, risk & AI platform

Before AI scales across a firm, it needs rails. We help your technology and compliance teams stand up secure model access, evaluation, and monitoring — and build the compliance tools that benefit from AI themselves.

  • Secure, logged internal LLM gateway
  • Evaluation suites on your own examples
  • E-comms surveillance triage
  • Restricted-list and MNPI checks
  • AI usage policy and controls
  • Vendor and model risk assessment

Questions

Common questions

What is a forward-deployed engineer?

An engineer who works directly with the people using the software instead of from a distant backlog. They learn the workflow first-hand, build quickly, and adjust based on what they see at the desk. It's how the best AI products actually get adopted.

What's a proactive agent?

An agent that runs on its own instead of waiting for someone to type a question. It starts from a schedule or an event, like a new filing, a data file, a limit breach, or an email. It does the first pass and brings the result to the right person. Anything with real consequences, like booking a fix or sending something outside the firm, waits for a human to approve it.

Where does our data go?

Nowhere you haven't approved. We deploy inside your cloud account or on-prem, use model providers under enterprise terms with zero data retention where available, and can run open-weight models entirely in your environment when needed.

Do you work on-site?

Yes, when it helps. Most engagements are hybrid: in person for the first week of shadowing and key milestones, remote with daily contact the rest of the time.

Which models and tools do you use?

Whatever fits your constraints. We're model-agnostic and work across the major frontier and open-weight models, and we build on your existing stack — Python, your data warehouse, Excel, Bloomberg, your OMS — rather than replacing it.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You own everything: code, prompts, evaluation sets, and infrastructure. We document it and train your team to run it. If you'd like us to stay on, the retainer model covers that.

Will you sign an NDA?

Of course — before the first substantive conversation. We're used to the confidentiality expectations of investment firms.
