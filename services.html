What we build for research desks

One set of research agents that every fund needs, plus a pack for your strategy. Each deployment starts with one workflow and one analyst, runs in your cloud, and is live on your coverage in weeks.

The core

What every desk gets

Agents that read what your fund already licenses, join it to your positions and coverage list, and report what changed since last time. Output goes where the team already works.

  • Pre-open brief by email, per analyst or per book
  • Broker notes compared with the prior note and consensus
  • Earnings-call and filing comparisons, quarter on quarter
  • Expert-call flags from your note-taker, with MNPI held back
  • Notes filed to the right company in your notes system
  • Model updates pushed into your Excel templates
Strategy packs

What changes by strategy

The core stays the same. What changes is which documents matter and what "changed" means. A pack adds the sources and checks for your strategy.

Alt data

  • Cohort and share analyses, rerun on each release

  • Entity resolution and ticker mapping
  • Natural-language access to your warehouse

Common questions

What is a forward-deployed engineer?

An engineer who works with the people using the software instead of from a distant backlog. They learn the workflow first-hand, build quickly and adjust to what they see at the desk.

Do our analysts have to learn a new tool?

No. Briefs arrive by email, notes land in the system you already use, and numbers go into your Excel templates. If you use Slack, Teams or Symphony, alerts can go there too.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your cloud account or on-prem. Model calls go only to providers you approve, under terms that forbid training, with zero data retention where the provider offers it. We can also use your own provider accounts, or models hosted entirely in your environment. See Security for the full terms.

Can you work with the data we already license?

Yes, and that's usually where we start. We build inside your environment and within your vendor agreements.

Do you share anything between clients?

No. General engineering experience carries over; your data, positions, prompts and outputs do not.

Do you work on-site?

Yes, when it helps. Most engagements are hybrid: in person for the first week and key milestones, remote with daily contact the rest of the time.

Which models and tools do you use?

Whatever fits your constraints. We work across the major frontier and open-weight models and build on your existing stack, such as Python, your data warehouse, Excel, Bloomberg and your OMS.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You keep the code, prompts, test sets and infrastructure, and we train your team to run them. Your data is returned and deleted, and we certify the deletion in writing. If you'd like us to stay on, the retainer covers that.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before the first substantive conversation.
