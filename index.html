Where your data goes.

your environment

Into your own cloud account, and no further than you approve. Each line below is a term in our client agreement.

  • Runs in your environment. We build and run everything in your cloud or on-prem. No copies on our systems unless you approve one in writing for a specific task.
  • Model calls on your terms. Only to providers you approve, under terms that forbid training, with zero retention where the provider offers it. Or to models hosted inside your account.
  • Never used for training. Your data and outputs are never used to train or improve any model, ours or anyone else's. The one exception is a model you ask us to tune that stays in your account.
  • Never shared. Nothing from your fund is used for another client, in any form, including anonymized.
  • Yours at the end. You own what we build for you, with a permanent license to any reusable parts of ours inside it. Your data is returned, then deleted, and we certify the deletion in writing.
