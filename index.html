How we work

Embedded, fast, and built to hand off.

  1. Week 0

    Sit with the desk

    We shadow your analysts, PMs, or ops team for a few days. We map where time goes and pick one workflow where AI can pay for itself quickly.

  2. Weeks 1–2

    Ship a first version

    A working tool in the hands of real users — inside your environment, on your data, with your security team in the loop from day one.

  3. Weeks 3–8

    Iterate at the desk

    Daily feedback, tight loops, and evaluations against your own examples. We harden it until the team relies on it without thinking.

  4. Ongoing

    Hand off or expand

    You get the code, documentation, and a team that knows how to run it. Many clients then point us at the next workflow.
